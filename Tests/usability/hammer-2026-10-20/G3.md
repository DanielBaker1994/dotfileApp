## Findings

### G3-1 · S2 · 2/2 clean repro (+2 more) · typed keys land in the notes drawer shell while state says pane=editor
Steps: Notes with terminal drawer open (do:toggle-terminal), focus nvim editor; `ctrl+j` (drawer), `ctrl+k` (editor); state.pane.focused == "editor", ring on editor; type `G` `o` `bash`.
Expected: keys reach nvim. Actual: nvim untouched (mode n, line unchanged), the text appears at the shell prompt (shots/G3-15-after-soak.png: `GObashGObashGobashGobash`). Same after ctrl+tab x2 + click Notes header icon (k4,k20). 4/4 cycles in the soak; state `responder` is SwiftTerm.LocalProcessTerminalView for both editor and terminal so state cannot tell them apart.
Hypothesis: pane focus model vs first responder disagree after the Ctrl+J/K hop and after a view round trip (re-activating Notes does not restore the nvim view as first responder). host.rs pane focus / host_vim.rs.

### G3-2 · S2 · 10/10 after ~10 notes · Notes sidebar rows stop switching the note (click and Return)
Steps: Cmd+N x8 (Untitled-3..10; sidebar then 13 rows after two Ctrl+N doc-1/doc-2), then click any other row (real left click, hover/outline shows) or ctrl+h + Up/Down + Return.
Expected: nvim switches to that note; state.views.notes.selection follows. Actual: nvim stays on the last note (verified `expand("%:t")` over RPC), `selection` stuck at the newest row, `pane.focused` stays "sidebar" (click_row would call focus_editor, so mouseDown/Select is not reaching/applying). Earlier in the same session with 3 notes a click on a row worked (opened Untitled-1). Open in Notes from Files, Cmd+N, and the Space-s-f finder all still switch notes fine, and a direct `:edit` over RPC works. Also `state.sidebarCursor` never moves with Up/Down (visual outline does).
Hypothesis: notes_sidebar_event Select -> NotesSurface::click_row/select_row (host.rs:7113, notes.rs:4154) no-ops once something (modified-externally buffer, E31/E35 nvim errors, many tabs) is true; I did not isolate the trigger. Not isolated: whether writing Untitled-5..10 externally while Untitled-10 was open is the trigger.
